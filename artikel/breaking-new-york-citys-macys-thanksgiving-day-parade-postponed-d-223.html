Forecasters say high winds on Thursday may spoil the annual march of huge balloons and have moved the parade to Saturday.

Newly introduced York City’s Macy’s Thanksgiving Day parade has been postponed due to excessively windy weather and will now be held on Saturday.

Sources indicate that forecasters say an impending storm on Thursday may spoil the street party, which is marked by a march of huge balloons. AccuWeather is forecasting winds of 12 to 25 mph (19 to 40 km/h) and rain later on Thursday.
