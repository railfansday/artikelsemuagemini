Concern that settlement to resolve suit against pair may stymie legal claims that involve child sexual abuse material.

As the clock ticks toward final settlement for a sex-trafficking-related lawsuit against Jeffrey Epstein’s accountant and attorney, some experts are expressing concern that the agreement might stymie survivors’ search for justice in other litigation involving child sexual abuse images.

According to recent reports, the suit involves Darren Indyke and Richard D Kahn, who for decades worked as Epstein’s personal lawyer and accountant respectively. Both men also serve as executors of Epstein’s estate. Indyke and Kahn have insisted they knew nothing about Epstein’s crimes and have rejected allegations of wrongdoing.
