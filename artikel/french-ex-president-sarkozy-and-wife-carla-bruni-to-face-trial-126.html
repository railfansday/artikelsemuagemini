Nicolas Sarkozy and his wife Carla Bruni-Sarkozy are to face trial next year over charges stemming from the former French president's conviction for conspiring to fund his election campaign with money from late Libyan dictator Muammar Gaddafi.

The charges centre on the retraction of the testimony of a key witness who had initially said he had evidence that could incriminate Sarkozy.

According to recent reports, sarkozy was given a five-year sentence in the case last September and became the first French ex-president to go to jail, even though he only spent 20 days behind bars. He has always denied doing anything wrong in the case and the appeals verdict is expected next Monday.

Since losing his re-election bid in 2012, Sarkozy has faced several criminal investigations.
