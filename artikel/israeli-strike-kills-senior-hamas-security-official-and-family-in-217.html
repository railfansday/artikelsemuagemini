An Israeli air strike in the Gaza Strip has killed a senior Hamas security official and three members of his family, according to local doctors and the Hamas-run interior ministry.

Mohammed Abu al-Ouf, 47, the head of Hamas's internal security service in northern Gaza, his wife and their two teenage daughters died when their apartment in Shati refugee camp in Gaza City was bombed at dawn, the ministry said.

The Israeli military said Abu al-Ouf was a "terrorist" and that he was eliminated "to remove the threat he posed".

On Tuesday, the overall commander of Hamas's internal security service was among 10 Palestinians reportedly killed in Israeli strikes.

Wednesday's strike in Shati caused extensive damage to the Abu al-Ouf family's apartment.

Photos demonstrated the exterior wall and several interior walls were destroyed, with rubble and broken furniture left scattered across the floor.

An interior ministry statement noted that Abu al-Ouf was killed along with his wife Noura, 41, and their daughters Yusra, 15, and Jana, 13.

It condemned the attack as a "heinous crime" and accused Israel of targeting security personnel in Gaza "in an effort to spread chaos within Palestinian society".

Hamas's internal security service is responsible for enforcing discipline, protecting the group's infrastructure and rooting out informants.

Israel's military noted in a statement that Abu al-Ouf was a "significant figure" who had served as "a key source of expertise" in efforts to restrict Israeli counter-terrorism operations in Gaza.

"His elimination is a blow to the apparatus's leadership capabilities and follows yesterday's elimination of his commander," it added.

On Tuesday, an Israeli drone strike in western Gaza City killed Naeem Abu Nahel, the head of Hamas's internal security service.

He had only been in the position since September, when his predecessor was captured in a raid in Gaza City by Israeli special forces.

Near daily Israeli strikes continue in Gaza despite a ceasefire agreed in October last year.

At least 1,679 people have been killed in Gaza over the past 13 months, according to the territory's Hamas-run health ministry, whose figures are considered reliable by the UN.

Israel states six of its soldiers have been killed in Gaza during the same period.

The Gaza war was triggered by the Hamas-led attack on southern Israel on 7 October 2023, when about 1,200 people were killed and 251 others were taken hostage.

Since then, more than 74,740 people have been killed in Israeli attacks in Gaza, the health ministry says.
