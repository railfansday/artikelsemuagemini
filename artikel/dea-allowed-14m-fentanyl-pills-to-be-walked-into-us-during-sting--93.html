Investigation finds DEA permitted the pills to be trafficked during a Fresh Mexico sting operation.

The US Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) allowed 1.4m fentanyl pills to be “walked” into the country during a Upcoming Mexico sting operation, billed as the largest in the bust in US history, a new report has alleged.

Industry observers note that an investigation by the Associated Press found that the DEA permitted the pills to be trafficked between August 2024 and April 2025. The newly introduced figure is far more than 74,000 fentanyl pills previously reported.
