The number of women in Ireland killed by men has surged in the past five years and risen especially sharply in the past 12 months, according to a study.

After falling for two decades, the number of violent deaths began going up in 2021 and has risen sharply this year, with 13 deaths so far, the report by Irish charity Women’s Aid stated on Wednesday.
