Many older Australians are dying before they can access home support, paying exorbitant fees, and being pushed into hospitals and residential care before they are ready because of a flawed federal government aged care program, a Senate inquiry has found.

Industry observers note that the inquiry is examining the impact of the Support at Home program, which rolled out in November 2025 and has been plagued by complaints of making care harder to access and more expensive. The inquiry was due to finish in November, but has been extended for a further year after receiving almost 300 submissions.
