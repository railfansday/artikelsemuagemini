Record-breaking king tides have prompted west coast authorities to warn residents to stay away from beaches.

The US west coast has seen record-breaking tides this week and authorities have urged residents to avoid beaches, but not everyone is heeding those warnings.

In a recent development, in Cannon Beach, Oregon, viral footage showed a man and his dog nearly getting swept away by a so-called king tide on Tuesday morning. The man had been standing on a staircase holding his leashed dog, who was walking in the sand, when the water suddenly surged, knocking him to his feet.
