Poll of parents of young children finds three-quarters think pressure from influencers has increased their anxiety.

Parents of children aged five and under say pressure from social media influencers to be “the perfect parent” is making it harder to raise a family, according to a survey.

Three-quarters (76%) of those who took part in the UK-wide poll agreed social media had increased anxiety around parenting, while three out of five (59%) complained of “impossible expectations” and “feeling judged”.
