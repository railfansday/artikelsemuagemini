Westminster council signs off on ‘cultural experience and exhibition’ at site where band last performed in public.

Fans of the Beatles will be able to stand on the Savile Row rooftop in London where the band held their final public performance, but any visitors hoping to experience what made the site a famed rock destination will be disappointed – because live music will be banned.

Westminster city council has voted on proposals to turn the grade-II * listed building at 3 Savile Row in central London into a seven-storey “cultural experience and exhibition” honouring the music history and legacy of the Fab Four.
