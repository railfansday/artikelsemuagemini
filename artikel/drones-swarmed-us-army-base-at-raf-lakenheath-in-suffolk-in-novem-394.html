Personnel told to take cover after ‘suspected unauthorised activity’ of six drones that were repelled but not recovered.

Drones reportedly swarmed a US army base in Suffolk earlier this month and were not recovered after being repelled.

Personnel at RAF Lakenheath were told to take cover on 13 November after “suspected unauthorised drone activity”. The US air force confirmed the so-called Charlie alert at the time, the second-highest threat level, but released few details. Sirens could be heard at the base. Iran is suspected of being involved.
