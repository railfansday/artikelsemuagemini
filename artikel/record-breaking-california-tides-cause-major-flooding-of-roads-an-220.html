Beaches close as Los Angeles and La Jolla see all-time high tides and major waves batter landmark pier in Pacifica.

Super El Niño weather brought record-breaking high tides up and down the California coast on Wednesday, sending seawater rushing into beachside communities and flooding roads and businesses.

As substantial waves battered the coast, the tip of a landmark pier in Pacifica, south of San Francisco, was slammed into the sea, local news outlets reported. The pier, which had once housed a favorite local cafe, had previously suffered structural damage and had been closed to the public, the San Francisco Chronicle reported, and collapsed after contact with ocean swells 12 times stronger than on an average November day.
