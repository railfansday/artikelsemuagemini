According to recent reports, “Two groups of hackers accessed Russia’s main statistics database last month and discovered a cache of 11m death certificates that revealed nearly 500,000 Russian losses in Ukraine since 2022. Among these were 1,000 – the equivalent of a whole regiment – who took their own lives.

Industry observers note that the documents show how the majority of soldiers come from poorer areas and ethnic minorities, drawn in by high salaries. There are also recruits from abroad and even those lured to Russia by offers of jobs. But for how long can Vladimir Putin keep this shocking death toll from the Russian people?”.
